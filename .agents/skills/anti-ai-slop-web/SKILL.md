# Anti-AI-Slop Web Design Standard

Follow these mandatory rules for every website, landing page, or business demo. Never deliver a page without rendering and visually inspecting BOTH desktop (`1280px`) and mobile (`390px`) screenshots against ALL THREE generations of AI slop below.

## 1. Hard Prohibitions (Instant Reject If Present)

### Gen-1 AI Slop (v0 / Tailwind / Lovable tells)
- **No letter-in-a-square placeholder logos** (e.g. `[S] Sleepadent`). Use a clean typographic wordmark or domain-authentic mark.
- **No twin side-by-side hero CTA buttons** (`[Solid Primary]` + `[Outlined Secondary]`). Use ONE clear primary action.
- **No duplicate primary CTA buttons** simultaneously visible in the navbar and hero.
- **No frosted-glass (`backdrop-filter: blur`) badges/pills** floating over photography (e.g. `● Disponible hoy`).
- **No symmetrical 3-column generic icon/feature boxes** with 2-word titles and interchangeable filler copy.
- **No "carditis"**: never wrap every block of content in rounded `6px–16px` shadowed white boxes over light gray backgrounds.
- **No purple/indigo gradients, radial glow blobs, gradient text clips, infinite marquee tapes, rotating circular SVG seals, fake IG story widgets, or flickering neon text.**

### Gen-2 AI Slop (Claude / "Anti-Slop Slop" tells)
- **No oversized monumental hero titles** (`60px` to `260px` `<h1>` or `50px+` `<h2>`). Keep commercial business `<h1>` strictly grounded in **`30px–46px`** and `<h2>` in **`22px–32px`**.
- **No colored italic keyword trick** inside a serif headline (`Atención dental *sin prisas*...`).
- **No monospace `01 ·` section prefixes with horizontal lines** (`01 · SERVICIOS ———`).
- **No hollow brochure clichés** (*seamless, elevate, unlock, empower, sin prisas*) or em-dash (`—`) overuse.

### Gen-3 AI Slop (Cookie-Cutter Wix / Durable / Bootstrap AI Builder tells)
- **NEVER reuse the same layout skeleton across multiple businesses** (changing only accent color and stock photos is instant slop). Every business must have its own **layout architecture, typography pairing, geometry, and physical/domain-native artifact**.
- **No default "dark utility topbar + 66px white navbar + full-bleed dark-scrimmed stock photo banner + gray section of rounded white cards"** template. That exact 4-band stack is the #1 fingerprint of cheap AI site builders.
- **No muddy dark gradient scrims smothering daylight photography** (especially food, salon, nail, retail, or clinic interiors). Show bright, unobstructed photographs beside clean typography instead of burying photos under dark overlays.
- **No giant blank/default Google Maps `<iframe>` rectangles** that fail to load cleanly or look like a generic Wix footer. Use a crisp structured location/hours block with a real direct Google Maps link.
- **No robotic SEO-bot headlines** (`"Corte clásico, fade y barba en Barbería Los Compas"`, `"Prendas únicas revisadas en La Percha"`). Write natural, direct human copy that sounds like the real owner speaking to a local customer.

## 2. Mandatory Craft Principles (What Real Studios Build)

1. **Start From a Physical, Domain-Native Metaphor ("Sin cajas, con formas propias"):**
   - **Taquería:** Printed counter menu + physical sticky **Comanda de pedido** pad (with real salsa/cebolla toggles and live total), paired with bright daylight food photography.
   - **Barbería:** Architectural hairline price ledger + **Libreta de turnos** slip (service, barber choice, time window).
   - **Estética:** Centered quiet editorial masthead + bright 3-photo daylight triptych + sharp `0px`-radius ruled two-column salon menu (no clunky checkboxes).
   - **Estudio de uñas:** Bright white studio opener with interactive **Forma + Largo** configurator right beside the manicure photo + borderless lookbook photo grid.
   - **Tienda de ropa / paca:** Indie retail header with live **Bolsa (N)** counter + borderless 3:4 lookbook rack with physical kraft hang-tags (`TALLA M`, `AGOTADA`, `EN BOLSA`) + boutique apartado receipt.
2. **Sharp Structural Discipline Instead of Carditis:** Use real typographic hierarchy, crisp `1px–2px` rules, generous alignment, and subtle surface shifts rather than wrapping every item in white rounded cards.
3. **Typography with Character & Restraint:** Pair at most ONE expressive local display font (e.g. `Fraunces`, `Cormorant Garamond`, `Newsreader`, `Schibsted Grotesk`, `Fredoka`) with ONE clean readable UI font (`Instrument Sans`, `Jost`, `DM Sans`, `Nunito`), plus mono (`DM Mono` / `Courier Prime`) strictly for tabular prices, times, or physical tags. Prefer local `.woff2` fonts whenever available.
4. **No Cluttered Sticky Bars on Initial Load:** Never stamp a permanent floating dark checkout bar across the bottom of the screen before the user interacts. Put interactive slips in-column on desktop and clean in-flow on mobile.
5. **Reliable WhatsApp Links:** ALWAYS use real `<a href="https://api.whatsapp.com/send?phone=521...&text=..." target="_blank" rel="noopener">` (or `https://web.whatsapp.com/send?...` for desktop outreach tools). NEVER rely on `window.open()` inside a `<button>` click.
6. **Zero `loading="lazy"` on Key Visible Media:** Avoid lazy-loading small thumbnails/cards that leave blank boxes on quick scroll or static captures.

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Delivery Verification

Before considering any page done:
1. Render and visually inspect BOTH **desktop (`1280px`)** and **mobile (`390px`)** screenshots.
2. Check ruthlessly for:
   - Does it look genuinely distinct from any other demo in the project?
   - Are ALL photos crisp, loaded, and unobstructed (no muddy dark scrims, no blank boxes)?
   - Are inline text labels properly stacked (`display: block`) with zero glued words?
   - Zero horizontal overflow, zero Gen-1 / Gen-2 / Gen-3 AI slop tells.
